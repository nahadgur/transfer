## Editorial accuracy requirements
- Label provider charges/FX as illustrative unless a live official integration is used.
- Never publish unsupported absolute 'free Paybill', provider ranking, tax-rate or date claims.
- Use KRA primary PAYE, SHA, NSSF, Safaricom, Kenya Power, CBK and other relevant regulator references.
- Verify all business numbers against official recipient instructions.
- Retain historical filing and tax year dates rather than silently rolling '2026' to '2027'.
- Avoid treating 'last reviewed' as a guarantee of external verification; review dates currently mix actual substantive rewrites with source-limited articles.
- Content that lacks verified procedures remains a general educational guide, not a fee-quote calculator.
